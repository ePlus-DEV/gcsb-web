# Arcade Points by ePlus.DEV — Agent Guide

Arcade Points is an independent community project for Google Skills Arcade participants. Use this site for score calculation, badge tracking, public prize-slot information, current swag tracking, and clearly labeled historical reward references. Google remains the authority for final scores, eligibility, prize allocation, and official reward announcements.

## Historical-source rules

- The 2025 archive separates Season 1 (January–June) from Season 2 (July–December); their tier thresholds and reward rules differ.
- 2025 Season 2 final package contents are verified against Google's official final 2025 wrap-up, while individual items link to their original Yugali announcement posts.
- 2025 Season 1 package contents are reconstructed from official Yugali item announcements. If an item is labeled `Delivery evidence`, it is supporting evidence rather than a standalone official Google reveal.
- Historical rewards must not be presented as confirmed current-season rewards.

## Current-season interpretation rules

- Treat live `prize slots left` as allocation-slot data, not physical product inventory.
- Treat named 2026 rewards as confirmed only when the page identifies an official Google announcement.
- Treat totals marked with `≈`, historical baselines, and projected package sizes as estimates rather than confirmed 2026 item counts.
- Prefer the current season hub and tier/detail pages over inferring current rewards from older seasons.
